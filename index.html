About

I am an M.Sc. student in Computer Science and Technology at the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), advised by Prof. Pietro S. Oliveto. My research lies in the runtime analysis of randomized search heuristics and evolutionary algorithms: understanding, rigorously, how long these algorithms take to find good solutions and why. My current work focuses on designing hyper-heuristics and establishing provable runtime guarantees for their performance, as well as the theoretical analysis of multi-objective evolutionary algorithms, in particular NSGA-III.

Seeking Ph.D. Positions: I'm about to graduate in Jun 2028 and am seeking Ph.D. positions in theoretical computer science, especially in randomized search heuristics and stochastic optimization.

Looking for collaborations: I am open to collaborations with talented researchers with similar interests. If you are interested, please feel free to reach out to me via email.
